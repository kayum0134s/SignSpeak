About SignSpeak

SignSpeak is a bilingual sign language translator that converts speech and text into sign language animations and videos.

✨ Features

  • 🎤 Voice Input: Speak naturally and convert to sign language
  • ⌨️ Text Input: Type in English or Hindi
  • 🇺🇸 ASL Support: American Sign Language with alphabet images
  • 🇮🇳 ISL Support: Indian Sign Language with Hindi phrase recognition
  • 🖼️ Gallery Mode: View signs as image cards
  • 🎬 Video Mode: Watch sign language videos
  • 📝 Fingerspelling: Automatic letter-by-letter spelling for unknown words

🌐 Supported Languages

  • English: Full alphabet, common words, and phrases
  • Hindi: 20+ common phrases (namaste, shukriya, etc.)

📚 How to Use

  1. Select your preferred sign language (ASL or ISL)
  2. Enter text by typing or using voice input
  3. Click "Translate to Signs"
  4. Choose Gallery or Video mode to view the translation

🎯 Technology

Built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3. Features speech recognition, text processing, and multimedia playback capabilities.
